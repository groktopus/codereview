## Status meanings and evidence rule

`MET_IMPLEMENTATION` means current-source code plus focused tests demonstrate the named deterministic boundary; it does not mean the entire product criterion is proven in production. `OBSERVED_OPERATION` means an exact-run observation demonstrates the behavior only for that case and source. `INCOMPLETE` means there is relevant evidence but a material part of the criterion remains unverified or unresolved. `MISSING` means the required capability/evidence is absent. `UNKNOWN` means the available evidence cannot establish the property. Multiple status terms in a cell keep these distinctions visible.
